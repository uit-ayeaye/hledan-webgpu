Both were downloaded from Poly Pizza (https://poly.pizza) and are used
unmodified except for normalisation performed at load time: each is scaled
per-axis to its real-world dimensions (bus 2.50 x 3.20 x 11.5 m, car
1.78 x 1.52 x 4.40 m), centred, and dropped onto the ground plane. See
src/legacy/props.js -> loadVehicleGeometry.

The original street furniture on this page — the street furniture, the tea shops, the
water-pot stands, the poles and cables, the lighting and the soundscape —
is generated procedurally in this repository and carries no third-party
licence. The map itself (models/hledan.glb) and the character models are
covered by the showcase's own terms.
