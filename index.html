A LITTLE CLOSER TO HOME

A living memory of Hledan.

Built around Thomas D. Lynn’s original Hledan Junction map. An atmospheric interpretation of the neighbourhood, with tea stalls, YBS buses, taxis, water pots, and the flyover we know by heart.

This release covers Hledan, not all of Yangon. Geometry and some textures are stylized and contain baked lighting. Sounds are synthesized, not field recordings.

Made with, and inspired by

Three.js WebGPU & TSL. HDR skies and asphalt by Poly Haven (CC0). Rendering research: Dan Greenheck’s Tidewater, WebGPU skill, and particle fluids. This is an independent implementation, not a complete port of those engines.

Bus by Poly by Google — CC BY 3.0. Car by Quaternius — CC0. Vehicle dimensions and liveries adapted at load time.
